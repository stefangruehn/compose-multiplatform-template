## Menu

The button at the top left opens the menu with every page of the app.

- Tap a page to open it. The menu closes and marks the page you are on.
- **Home** and **About** have their pages indented under them. The arrow at the end of each folds those pages away or shows them again; the menu opens that way next time too.
- On a short screen, the menu scrolls.
- **Settings** opens the settings, as the gear at the top right does.
- **Exit**, below the line, closes the app.
- The cross at the top, a tap beside the menu, a swipe to the left or the back gesture close it.

## Getting around

- **Home** shows a card for each page under it, **About** one for each page about the app; tap a card to open it.
- The back gesture leads to the page above: from a page under **About** to **About**, from any other to **Home**.
- Every page opens where you left it, also after the app has been closed.

## Settings

- **Start page** opens the app on **Home** or on the page you left.
- **Theme** follows the phone, or keeps the app light or dark.
